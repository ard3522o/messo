# Smart Mess Management — explainer video

**Format:** 16:9 website walkthrough, about 60 seconds, calm confident English voiceover, futuristic but readable interface, subtle electronic underscore.

## Voiceover

Welcome to IIIT Bhubaneswar’s Smart Mess Management System: one place to keep everyday dining operations clear and connected.

Students can sign in to a personal dashboard, review meal attendance, and check in using a meal QR code. The weekly menu brings breakfast, lunch, and dinner into one simple schedule, with search, CSV export, and print built in.

Mess teams can review feedback and reports, while profiles and announcements keep important information easy to find. Behind the interface, a React website connects to an Express API and MongoDB data store.

Smart Mess Management — a clearer way to coordinate the campus dining experience.

## Production notes

- Use a warm, clear narrator; moderate pace and neutral Indian English pronunciation.
- Keep UI text faithful to the website; do not invent features or metrics.
- Capture actual browser screens for the dashboard, QR check-in, menu, feedback, and reports. Use subtle camera pans and zooms; avoid replacing legible product UI with generated fake text.
- Keep captions high-contrast and readable. Add low-volume ambient electronic music under narration.
